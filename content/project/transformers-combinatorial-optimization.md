---
title: Applying Transformers to Combinatorial Problems
summary: "Master's thesis, in progress: extending transformer models that construct solutions for the traveling salesman and permutation flow shop problems to new combinatorial problems."
start: 2025-09
end: 2027-06
---

Master's thesis at CTU FEE in Prof. Zdeněk Hanzálek's group, started as a semester project in the 2025/26 winter semester and due in 2027. Transformer models, first built for natural language, can construct solutions to combinatorial optimization problems such as the traveling salesman problem and the permutation flow shop. The thesis starts from Bresson and Laurent's transformer for the traveling salesman problem and an existing transformer for the permutation flow shop, identifies further combinatorial problems the approach could solve, designs the model extensions they need, and validates them with computational experiments. In progress; no results yet.
