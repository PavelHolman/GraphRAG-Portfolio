Machine Learning Prague 2026, 4 to 6 May 2026 in Prague. Pavel attended the conference and two hands-on workshops: "Unlocking Large-Scale Resilient Model Training with SageMaker HyperPod", on training large models on AWS SageMaker HyperPod clusters, and "Recommender Systems in the Age of Generative AI". The 2026 talks gave a current picture of applied machine learning in production: moving from simple retrieval-augmented generation to enterprise AI agents, evaluating agents, the Model Context Protocol, scaling LLM inference, embedding models and hybrid search, sequential recommendations for search ranking, time-series forecasting, and why good models fail after deployment. Workshop and conference learning, not production use.
