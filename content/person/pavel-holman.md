Pavel Holman is a software engineer in Prague studying for a master's degree in Artificial Intelligence at CTU's Faculty of Electrical Engineering, expected June 2027, after a bachelor's in AI at CTU's Faculty of Information Technology. He co-founded DeelsCorp in 2024 and poqet in 2026 and has been the principal engineer of both: at Deels he led a four-person team across mobile apps, a Kotlin backend, APIs and infrastructure; at poqet he leads a three-person team building SLIM, a lab operations platform, and owns the agentic development tooling around it. He has worked part-time at Merica since 2020 on C# business software, including employee rostering with constraint programming. His bachelor's thesis built an AutoML pipeline forecasting day-ahead and intraday electricity prices from market, weather and grid data. He reached the ICPC Central Europe Regional Contest finals in 2024 and 2025 and the ICPC European Championship finals in 2026.

Strongest skills: TypeScript with Effect, Kotlin, and agentic engineering (Claude Code harnesses, guardrails and evaluations). He prefers practical impact over theory: a working implementation or proof of concept, typed and explicit systems, and claims backed by evidence, which is why this portfolio answers from a knowledge graph with citations. Interests: programming languages (Kotlin, Swift, Rust), functional programming and system design, algorithms, agentic engineering, and neuro-symbolic AI and automated experimentation as areas he is curious about rather than expert in.
